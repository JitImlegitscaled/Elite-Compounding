# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: pharmacy owners and pharmacists-in-charge (PIC) of sterile compounding facilities in Florida, evaluating consultancies ahead of a USP <797> / <800> inspection or after a deficiency notice. They are clinically trained, allergic to fluff, and visit the site at the point of high-stakes decision making.

Secondary: compliance officers and facility managers actively triaging deficiencies, looking for a partner credible enough to defend findings to a regulator.

Context of use: desktop during business hours, occasionally mobile while on-site at the cleanroom. They arrive skeptical, scan credentials first, read for evidence of inspector-grade rigor, and decide within minutes whether the firm is worth a call.

Job to be done: confirm in under sixty seconds that Elite Compounding Agency is the consultancy regulators take seriously, then convert to an inspection-readiness consultation.

## Product Purpose

Lead-generation site for a high-ticket B2B compliance consultancy. Success is a qualified inquiry from a sterile compounding facility, not a newsletter signup or a downloaded PDF. Every section either builds regulatory credibility or moves the visitor toward the consultation form.

## Brand Personality

The firm regulators recommend. Sharp, confident, authoritative. Three words: regulator-grade, exacting, decisive.

Voice: direct, technical when technical is warranted, never marketing-speak. Uses the right nouns (USP <797>, USP <800>, beyond-use date, ISO 5, environmental monitoring) because the audience does. Does not explain what those terms mean unless the explanation is itself the value.

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel they have found the adult in the room. Not warmth. Not relief. Confidence that this is the firm whose name an inspector recognizes.

## Anti-references

Things this site must not feel like:

- Generic SaaS landing page. No cream backgrounds, no soft pastel illustrations, no "Trusted by 10,000+ pharmacies" social-proof carousel of logo wallpaper.
- Agency-flashy. No layered gradients on every section, no animated blobs, no decorative motion that exists to prove the designer can animate.
- Medical-marketing stock-photography site. No smiling pharmacists in lab coats holding tablets, no teal-and-white "modern healthcare" palette.
- Startup pitch deck. No hero metric template (big number, small label, gradient accent). No "the future of compounding" framing.
- Lawyer / consulting boilerplate. Not navy-and-gold seriousness for its own sake. Authority comes from specificity, not stuffiness.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over claims.** Specifics (USP chapter numbers, certifications, regulatory bodies, facility types) carry weight; adjectives do not. Every section should show, not tell.
2. **Earn the visitor's scan.** The audience makes the call in the first viewport. Hierarchy, credentials, and the single primary CTA must land before anything decorative.
3. **Inspector-grade craft.** The audience is trained to spot sloppiness. Misaligned grids, inconsistent spacing, and lazy copy are evidence the firm cuts corners. Treat polish as the proof of the pitch.
4. **One voice of authority.** No competing visual moods. The site is one tightly-controlled document, not a collage of section styles.
5. **Restraint signals seniority.** A junior firm proves itself with effects. A senior firm trusts the work. Reduce ornament, sharpen typography, let the white space carry the weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The audience skews 35–65, includes prescription-glasses wearers, and reads dense technical content. Practical implications:

- Body text never below 16px; primary reading copy at 17–18px with line-height 1.6–1.7.
- All text against tinted neutrals, never pure white on pure white or pure black on pure black; contrast verified against the cobalt palette.
- Focus indicators clearly visible (this is a form-driven site; keyboard users must be able to navigate the consultation form).
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for all reveal and scroll animations.
- Touch targets 44×44 minimum on mobile; the inquiry CTA must remain reachable with one thumb.
